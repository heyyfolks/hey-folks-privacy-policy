Privacy Policy

For Heyy Folks Lead Intelligence · Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains how Heyy Folks (“we”, “us”, or “our”) intends to handle information in connection with Heyy Folks Lead Intelligence, a business research and prospect-management tool (the “Service”). This page is a general policy for the Service and should be updated if its actual features or data practices change.

1. Information the Service may handle

  • Information you provide: search criteria, business or prospect details you enter, notes, tags, and other information you choose to save.
  • Business and professional information: information that is publicly available or that you are authorised to access and use for legitimate business research.
  • Connected-service information: if you choose to connect a third-party service, the Service may access only the information and permissions that you authorise and that the provider makes available through its approved integration.
  • Basic technical information: hosting or security services may process limited technical logs needed to operate and protect the Service.

2. How information may be used

Information may be used to support business research, organise prospect records, maintain user-requested notes, operate and secure the Service, troubleshoot issues, and comply with legal obligations. We do not intend to sell personal information or use it for unrelated advertising.

3. LinkedIn and other third-party services

LinkedIn is a separate service operated by LinkedIn Corporation. The Service is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn. Any LinkedIn integration will be used only after the required approvals and permissions are granted, and only in ways permitted by LinkedIn’s applicable terms and policies. We do not ask users to provide their LinkedIn password to this Service. Connecting an app does not itself grant access to data or features that the third-party provider has not authorised.

Third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms. Please review them before connecting an account.

4. Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by hosting, database, security, or integration providers only as needed to operate the Service, or disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect rights and safety. We do not intend to sell or rent prospect information to third parties.

5. Storage and security

We aim to use reasonable safeguards to protect information. No internet-based system can be guaranteed completely secure. Information may be stored by the hosting or database providers used by the Service, subject to their own terms and security practices.

6. Retention and deletion

Information should be kept only for as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer period is required by law. If you need information corrected or deleted, contact us using the link below. Deletion from this Service may not delete information held independently by a third-party provider.

7. Your choices

You should only add information you are entitled to use, keep prospect records relevant to a legitimate business purpose, and respect applicable privacy, marketing, and platform rules. You may stop using connected services or revoke permissions through the relevant provider’s account settings.

8. Children

The Service is intended for business use and is not designed for children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as the Service develops. The “Last updated” date above indicates when this page was most recently revised. Material changes should be reflected here before they apply.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the project owner through the Heyy Folks GitHub profile.
